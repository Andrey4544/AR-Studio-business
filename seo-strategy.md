# AR Studio SEO Strategy

## Objective

AR Studio will compete for **branded discovery**, **local commercial intent**, and **service-led informational intent**. The primary commercial objective is to increase qualified consultation and quote requests from businesses in Plovdiv and across Bulgaria, not to chase an unqualified promise of a universal first-place ranking. Google states that no SEO change automatically produces a first-place result; helpful content, accessibility to crawlers, and genuine prominence improve the opportunity to appear and perform in search over time.[1]

## Implementation Principles

The home page will become the authoritative overview for **уеб дизайн и изработка на сайтове в Пловдив**, with a clearer H1 and visible service/location language. A new local landing page will give this primary commercial intent its own useful, non-duplicative destination. It will explain AR Studio’s local process, services, service area, project suitability, and next steps rather than repeating generic marketing copy.

Metadata, canonicals, Open Graph URLs, structured-data entities, the sitemap, and the robots file will be standardized to the `https://www.ar-studio.site` canonical domain. Each important indexable route will have a distinct, accurate title and description. The new page will be internally linked from site navigation, the homepage, the services page, and the footer so users and crawlers can understand its importance.

The content programme will prioritize original, decision-helping resources for local buyers. The first supporting article will address how a Plovdiv business can choose a web-design partner and prepare a web project. It will include practical criteria, service expectations, and internal links to the local landing page and services. This follows Google’s people-first content guidance rather than trying to repeat isolated keywords.[1]

## Local Authority Actions After the Site Work

| Action | Why it matters | Owner or access required |
|---|---|---|
| Verify and complete the Google Business Profile | Google states that complete, accurate, verified business information improves eligibility and relevance for local search.[2] | AR Studio owner access |
| Use the same business name, phone, website, service area, hours, and real social links everywhere | Consistent entity information helps users and platforms identify the same business. | AR Studio team |
| Invite genuine clients to leave Google reviews and answer each review professionally | Google identifies reviews, replies, and prominence as local-visibility signals.[2] | AR Studio team |
| Publish original project stories and seek legitimate links or mentions from clients, partners, and local business communities | Google explains that links and useful promotion support discovery; artificial link schemes are not part of this strategy.[1] | AR Studio team |
| Verify Search Console and submit the XML sitemap | Search Console allows sitemap submission, URL inspection, index coverage review, and query-level measurement.[3] | Google Search Console access |
| Add GA4 conversion tracking for consultation, contact, and quote completion | Enables monthly decisions based on qualified lead behaviour rather than ranking guesses. | GA4 property access |

## Measurement

The first review point should occur after Google has had time to crawl and process the changes. Google notes that changes can take from hours to months and recommends assessing effects over weeks rather than expecting immediate rank changes.[1] The performance dashboard should track indexed pages, branded impressions, non-branded impressions for local commercial terms, clicks, consultation-form starts, completed contact/quote submissions, and qualified leads.
